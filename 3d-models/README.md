# Note on the Current Status of the Files

The provided files are still at an early stage of development. They should therefore be considered preliminary and are not yet approved for final review or production.

In particular, the tolerances have not yet been fully defined and may still be inaccurate or inconsistent in some areas. A detailed review and final validation of the tolerances will be carried out in a later step.

Please use the files with appropriate caution and do not treat them as final versions.
